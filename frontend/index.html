About Our Portal

Losing personal belongings on campus can be stressful. This portal provides a common platform where students can report lost or found items, search for belongings, and submit claims to recover their items.

What Can You Do?

Everything you need to find or return belongings on campus.

Search Items

📋

Track Your Claims

Submit claims and check their approval status.

How It Works

1. Register or log in to your account.

2. Report a lost or found item.

3. Search for items and submit a claim.

4. Receive notifications about your claim.
